About Us

Our Story

Your Playmate Sock Dolls, LLC was founded with a simple but powerful mission: to bring smiles, warmth, and comfort to children across the country through handcrafted, soft, and lovable sock dolls.

Why We Started

Inspired by a personal story of hope and healing, our founder began sewing sock dolls for children in local hospitals. The joy and connection those dolls brought sparked the idea for this heartfelt business.

Who We Serve

We work closely with hospitals, care facilities, and individual families to ensure that every child receives a custom-made companion designed to provide comfort during tough times.

Our Team

Our team is made up of passionate crafters, compassionate volunteers, and dedicated supporters who believe that a soft smile can make a big difference.

What Makes Us Unique

Every sock doll is handmade with care, safety-tested, and infused with love. We focus on quality, compassion, and customization to meet the needs of each child we serve.
